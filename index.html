Short clips, posted on your schedule.

Sinag Automation is a desktop app that runs on its owner's own computer. It turns long videos into short vertical clips with captions, and posts the clips the owner approves to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts, on a schedule the owner sets.

How it works

  1. Make clips from your own videos, or videos you have permission to use.
  2. Review each clip and add the ones you want to the publish queue.
  3. Connect your TikTok or YouTube account with the platform's official sign-in.
  4. The app uploads each clip at its scheduled time. On TikTok it can send the clip to your inbox as a draft, so you post it yourself from the TikTok app.

Your accounts stay yours

The app only posts what you queue, only to accounts you connect, and you can disconnect an account at any time. It does not read your messages, followers or other people's data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
